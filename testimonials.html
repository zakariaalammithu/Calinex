What UI/UX design services does Wavespace offer?

Wavespace offers full-service UI/UX design services spanning the entire product lifecycle, from initial concept to launch and beyond. As a UX design agency for growing startups, the studio organizes its work into four stages: Think, Design, Ship, and Scale.

Think covers UX research, UX audits, and usability testing, the discovery work that grounds every decision in real user behavior, not guesswork.

Design is the core: UI/UX design, SaaS design, website and mobile app design, AI UI/UX, wireframing, and design systems built to grow with the product.

Ship closes the gap most agencies leave open: Webflow development and MVP development for web, SaaS, and AI products. Figma to live, one team.

Scale keeps the product converting after launch: CRO, UI/UX consultancy, and ongoing design partnerships.

That's what a full-service UI/UX design agency looks like: product design services from idea to launch, including UX UI design services for SaaS, fintech, and AI founders backed by YC, a16z, Sequoia, and Techstars.

Is Wavespace a good fit for early-stage startups?

Yes. Wavespace is a UX design agency for early-stage startups, with one honest caveat: the fit is strongest for funded teams. The studio works best with Seed to Series B founders who have a raise behind them and a product to build, not pre-funding ideas still hunting for a concept.

For an early-stage startup, that means MVP design for startups that ships fast: turn an idea into an investor-ready product, validate with users, and walk into the next round with something real. Many clients are YC-backed or sit in portfolios at a16z, Sequoia, and Techstars.

Startup product design here runs the full path, UX research to confirm the problem, UI/UX and SaaS design to build the product, Webflow or MVP development to ship it live. Product design for funded startups, start to finish.

If you're a funded founder past the idea stage, Wavespace is a strong design partner for startup founders. Pre-funding, pre-product? The team points you somewhere better, honest fit over a quick yes.

Can Wavespace handle enterprise and B2B SaaS product design?

Yes. Wavespace is a B2B SaaS design agency built for exactly this: dense, multi-user platforms where the workflows are complex and the stakes are high. Enterprise UX design and SaaS product design are core scope, not a side service.

Enterprise and B2B work means complex workflows: dashboards with hundreds of states, admin and billing layers, role-based permissions, and cross-platform consistency across web, tablet, and mobile. The studio handles all of it with design systems that keep the product coherent as it grows, an interface built to scale, not patched together.

This is where the embedded model earns its keep. Wavespace plugs into your internal product team like staff, Slack, standups, and shared Figma, so engineers treat the design file as production spec by week two.

Proof: HeyGen's design system reached 10M+ users; Delve's B2B analytics dashboard cut churn 18% after launch; a $250K enterprise engagement is in flight now.

For enterprise B2B SaaS design, UX design for complex SaaS platforms, or product design for B2B software teams, yes, Wavespace handles it.

Which industries does Wavespace design for?

Wavespace designs across six core industries, with deep, industry-specific UX design in fintech, healthtech, and B2B SaaS. The studio matches design patterns to each sector's rules and user behavior instead of reusing one generic template.

The six:

  • Finance & Fintech — fintech UX design for dashboards and flows users trust with money. Regulated product design, built for compliance.
  • EdTech & HealthTech — healthcare app design and edtech UX for patients, students, and admins on any device.
  • SaaS & B2B Platforms — dense team tools that stay fast as workflows grow.
  • E-Commerce & DTC — DTC e-commerce journeys that move users from browse to checkout in fewer steps.
  • Web3, AI & Emerging Tech — web3 design and AI interfaces that make complex tech feel simple.
  • Hospitality & Legal Services — dependable, low-friction products for service-led businesses.

Beyond the core six, the team also ships proptech and real estate products. As an industry-specialised design agency, Wavespace works fluently across regulated industries, a UX design agency for fintech and healthcare that treats compliance, trust, and edge cases as the job. UX design for regulated industries is core scope.

Does Wavespace design AI products and AI-powered platforms?

Yes, AI products are central to what Wavespace builds, not a trend it bolted on. As an AI UX design agency, the studio designs and ships the interfaces that make AI-powered platforms usable: the layer between a model and the person using it.

AI product design here covers the parts founders underestimate. AI onboarding that earns trust on first use. Generative AI UX where outputs are editable, explainable, and easy to act on. 

AI workflow design that turns a raw machine learning interface into something a non-technical user can run. The work spans AI SaaS dashboards, agent tools, and consumer AI apps.

It doesn't stop at design. AI MVP development means Wavespace ships the live product too- an AI UX design and development agency, one team from Figma to launch.

Proof: the studio has shipped for HeyGen (AI video, 10M+ users), Kodezi (AI coding assistant, raised Series A during the engagement), and Persana AI. As a design agency for AI products and a partner for UX design for AI startups, this is core scope.

Do you offer development or design only?

Both. Wavespace is a design and development agency, not a design-only studio. The team designs the product and ships it live, so there's no handoff gap where momentum and conversion usually leak out.

On the build side, that means MVP development services for web, SaaS, and AI products, plus Webflow development (Certified Partner) and Framer development (Enterprise Partner) for marketing sites and product front-ends. 

Need a store? Shopify development. Need speed without engineers? No-code development. Need something custom? A full-stack build. The point is one team from design to ship. Most agencies hand you a Figma file and disappear; your engineers then reverse-engineer it, and the design degrades on the way to production. 

Wavespace closes that gap with UI UX design and development services under one roof.

That's the whole model: an agency that designs and builds MVPs, runs CRO after launch, and stays on to grow the product. A design and development agency under one roof means Figma to live in weeks, not a relay race between two vendors.

How does Wavespace improve conversion rates?

Wavespace improves conversion rates by treating launch as the start, not the finish; the studio designs, ships, then optimizes what real users do. As a conversion rate optimization agency, it runs CRO services as a measured loop, not a one-time redesign.

The method is concrete. Funnel optimisation starts by mapping where users drop. Then cut onboarding friction, the dead clicks, extra steps, and confusing states that kill signups. 

Rebuild high-intent screens with conversion design, and prove the change with A/B testing instead of opinion. Heatmaps and session data (via Microsoft Clarity) show where attention and frustration actually land.

This works across the funnel: landing page conversion for first-touch traffic, activation flows for product onboarding, and engagement uplift deeper in. A UX redesign to increase conversions only counts when the numbers move.

The proof is in the work: a 300%+ average conversion uplift across projects. As a conversion rate optimisation agency for SaaS, Wavespace ships CRO design services to lift signups, not just prettier screens.
