Getting started

Learn about our approach, expertise, and how we help businesses build better digital products.

What does Wavespace actually help clients build?

Wavespace helps founders and businesses to build next-generation digital products, including SaaS platforms, mobile applications, websites, MVPs, and design systems through innovative UX research. Wavespace also helps by providing services like Webflow, Framer, MVP development, and turning ideas into scalable products that launch 3X faster.

Is Wavespace the right fit if I only have an idea and no product spec yet?

Yes, Wavespace is a perfect place if you have only an idea to get started. Wavespace can help you to shape your vision through discovery, UX strategy, user flow, and wireframes.

Our team has worked with multiple founders (YC included) to reduce uncertainty and elucidate product requirements before design or development. Wavespace has worked on the Kodezi SaaS design system, and the user experience redesign has helped them close a YC seed round with $1.8M.

Can Wavespace help me validate an idea before I spend on full development?

Yes, Wavespace has helped startups validate their ideas with our UX research process, including wireframes, MVP building, and clickable prototypes. Our approach includes gathering user feedback, testing assumptions, and refining product scope before the full investment. This will help you reduce risk and avoid unnecessary costs.

Should I hire a UI/UX agency before development starts?

Yes, starting with UI UX design agency before development is a great way to define the scope, product structure, and interface early. Another advantage of hiring a design agency before development is that a well-scoped product is easier to develop, test, and delivers a better user experience from the start.

What kind of founders, startups, or teams usually work with Wavespace?

Wavespace partners with startup AI & SaaS founders, SaaS companies, fintech businesses, and growth products that are looking for experienced UI UX design. Whether someone is building an MVP, launching a new product, enterprise platform, law firm design, or scaling an existing platform. Wavespace is the #1 agency to create high-converting, intuitive digital experiences for business growth.

Do I need a full redesign, or can Wavespace improve only one part of my product?

Not all design work needs to be a redesign. Wavespace understands what founders and businesses require. We deliver the best work possible because we don’t function as a traditional design agency. We become the 4th wheel of your vehicle.

As a partner, we help to fill the gap you have. We provide full redesign services when you need them, and we also provide design services to improve your product. We can build a design system for you and work on an existing design system to make it better.

What should I prepare before booking a discovery call with Wavespace?

We offer a free discovery call without pay. We don’t pitch or sell in this call. In this call, you have the floor. You talk, and we listen. You should be prepared to help us have the general idea and an understanding of your project.

Focus on your goals, target audience, timeline, and budget. If you have a specific or existing branding, a website, or a requirement, feel free to share them with us. Don’t worry if you don’t have everything figured out yet. That's exactly what the discovery call is for. Our team will discuss your ideas and help you out.

Can Wavespace help AI products feel usable and trustworthy for real users?

Many AI products have powerful technology but confusing user experiences. We help simplify AI workflows, explain outputs clearly, improve onboarding, design better prompts and actions, and create interfaces where users know what the AI is doing, why it matters, and what to do next.

Do you design for ecommerce brands and conversion-focused websites?

For ecommerce, design is not only about looking good. It needs to help users discover products, trust the brand, understand value, move through checkout, and complete purchases with less friction. We focus on both visual quality and conversion clarity.

Can Wavespace work on regulated or complex industries with heavy workflows?

Yes. Wavespace can work on complex products, regulated industries, and workflow-heavy platforms.

These products often involve multiple user roles, permissions, data-heavy screens, compliance needs, and complicated processes. We help simplify the experience through better information architecture, user flows, dashboard structure, design systems, and clear interaction patterns.

How do you adapt design decisions to different industries instead of using one generic style?

Wavespace does not use one generic design style for every industry. We adapt each design decision based on your users, market, business goals, product type, and industry expectations.

Process

Our step-by-step approach from discovery and research to design, delivery, and launch.

What happens after I book a discovery call with Wavespace?

After you book our free discovery call, we’ll contact you and conduct a meeting to discuss your project details. (Example: Challenges, timeline, budget, and requirements). The goal of our discovery call is to understand your vision.

We’ll ask questions regarding your business and recommend the best approaches. If we’re a great fit for you, you will receive a proposal with scope and next steps to get started.

What does your design process look like from research to launch?

Our design process is simple and collaborative. First, we start by understanding your project, business, users, and goals. Next, we conduct thorough market research and create user flow and wireframes. You will stay connected in every step.

After that, we design high-fidelity screens and test user experience. We start designing once everything is set. We design not only for you but also for your developers. After design delivery, we support developer handoff and stay connected through launch to ensure everything works as planned.

How long does a typical website, app, or MVP design project take?

Every project is different. Project time depends on scope and size. But most website projects take between 2–6 weeks, while web apps, mobile app design, and MVPs typically run 4–12 weeks. This is the average project timeline. During our discovery call, we will discuss more about the realistic timeline based on your project needs.

When will I see the first concepts or designs?

You’ll see the first wireframe and design concepts within the first 3–5 days after onboarding. We believe in sharing work early so that you can provide feedback before we move on to the full visual design.

How involved will I be during the project?

As much or as little as you want to stay involved. Our team will regularly update you with check-ins, process review, milestone and design updates, so you know every design decision we take.

You value your feedback and it is most important to us, but our project manager and lead designer also can do the heavy lifting so you can stay focused on running your business.

How do you handle feedback, revisions, and scope changes?

Feedback is a daily part of our design process. We include revision rounds to refine design so that we can ensure our work meets your expectations. We’re also flexible for scope changes mid-work. If new features or requirements are added beyond initial scope, we’re happy to discuss those changes.

Do you support developer handoff, QA, and post-launch optimization?

Yes. We deliver work with organized design files, documentation, and post-support for developers. We also help design QA to ensure the final work matches our design, and offer recommendations based on user feedback to improve after launch.

Every project is different. Project time depends on scope and size. But most website projects take between 2–6 weeks, while web apps, mobile app design, and MVPs typically run 4–12 weeks. This is the average project timeline. During our discovery call, we will discuss more about the realistic timeline based on your project needs.

When will I see the first concepts or designs?

You’ll see the first wireframe and design concepts within the first 3–5 days after onboarding. We believe in sharing work early so that you can provide feedback before we move on to the full visual design.

How involved will I be during the project?

As much or as little as you want to stay involved. Our team will regularly update you with check-ins, process review, milestone and design updates, so you know every design decision we take.

You value your feedback and it is most important to us, but our project manager and lead designer also can do the heavy lifting so you can stay focused on running your business.

How do you handle feedback, revisions, and scope changes?

Feedback is a daily part of our design process. We include revision rounds to refine design so that we can ensure our work meets your expectations. We’re also flexible for scope changes mid-work. If new features or requirements are added beyond initial scope, we’re happy to discuss those changes.

Do you support developer handoff, QA, and post-launch optimization?

Yes. We deliver work with organized design files, documentation, and post-support for developers. We also help design QA to ensure the final work matches our design, and offer recommendations based on user feedback to improve after launch.

Can Wavespace help me validate an idea before I spend on full development?

Should I hire a UI/UX agency before development starts?
