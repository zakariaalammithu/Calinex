Start your UX audit

Heuristic Evaluation

Spot design issues before they slow your users down. We evaluate your product against usability principles like clarity, error prevention, and feedback. This gives you a fast, expert-driven snapshot of what’s hurting the experience and where to start fixing it.

Request heuristic evaluation

UI/UX Design Review

Identify what’s helping and what’s hurting. We examine every page to review its layout, pictures, hierarchy, space allocation, and user interactions. You’ll know what’s not the right fit and how to address it in record time.

Get UI/UX review

User Journey & Task Flow Analysis

Discover where users are not progressing or becoming unhappy. We analyze the main user journeys of your product and check if they match actual user actions. So, you can streamline your process, lead people better, and direct them correctly.

Request user journey analysis

Usability Testing

See what your users need, even if they don’t speak up. Our team gives your product to real people to find any problems or confusion it may be causing. As they use the product, their immediate feelings shape their decisions, so the updates are useful, not just appealing.

Discover usability testing service

Heatmap & Analytics Audit

Track the areas users point their mouse at, scroll through, stop at, or leave without clicking. We turn to Hotjar, GA4, or Mixpanel to gather insights from the masses. You can redirect your redesign to make your product useful as it stands, not as it should be.

Get heatmap audit

Competitor UX Benchmarking

Find out what makes your industry’s top performers successful. We look at your product’s UX side by side with important competitors and category examples. The result? Ideas you can use and outshine your competition, not only follow their lead.

Request competitor benchmarking

Prioritized UX Audit Report

Know what to fix first and why. We compile all findings into a single, clear document, ranked by impact and effort. You provide your team with a clear guide pointing toward business outcomes, not only design updates.

What does a UX audit include?

A UX audit is a deep dive into how users feel and interact with your website, app, or digital product. It’s about spotting trouble areas and finding ways to make the user experience (UX) smoother, faster, and more engaging.

A strong ux audit services and solutions package looks at everything—from how pages load to how easy it is for users to find what they need. It includes UI/UX audit services, heatmaps, user flows, interface analysis, accessibility checks, and conversion rate optimization. Because even the smallest friction points can push customers away.

Think about it, if a visitor lands on your ecommerce site or homepage and struggles to check out, that’s lost sales. If your b2b SaaS dashboard is confusing, users won’t stick around. A ux audit agency helps you fix these gaps by analyzing user pain points, comparing your design to industry best practices, and offering custom solutions.

At CALINEX, we work with startups, enterprises, and small businesses to boost engagement and drive real results. If you need a full redesign or just a fine-tune, our team of UX specialists digs deep to help your brand stand out in the digital space. Because a great user experience isn’t a luxury—it’s what keeps customers coming back.

If your website, app, or digital product isn’t bringing the results you expect, a ux audit service can help you figure out why. Maybe users leave too soon, sales aren’t growing, or people struggle to use your interface. These are signs that something isn’t working, and that’s where a ux audit agency steps in.

Think about your ecommerce site, b2b platform, or startup app. Are users dropping off before checkout? Are support tickets piling up? A ui ux audit service looks at real data, maps user behavior, and spots weak points that could be hurting your business. Even the best brands and enterprises hit roadblocks, but a focused ux audit services and solutions package gives clear fixes to boost engagement and sales.

We’ve seen small businesses and large companies miss out on growth just because of hidden UX issues. The good news? They’re fixable. With expert consultants and specialists, you get a game plan to make your homepage, product, or platform smoother and more intuitive.

Because a frustrating user experience isn’t just bad design—it’s lost customers. And in today’s digital world, you can’t afford that.

What is the process of UX audit?

A UX audit is a step by step process that delves into how users interact with your website, app, or platform. The goal? The primary goal is to uncover roadblocks, improve the user experience, and boost conversions. A great ux audit services and solutions package follows a structured plan to find what’s holding your business back.

First, a ux audit agency collects data. This means looking at b2b analytics, heatmaps, user behavior, and conversion rates. If people are dropping off your ecommerce checkout or leaving your homepage too fast, that’s a red flag.

Next, a deep ui ux audit services review checks your interface, navigation, content clarity, and accessibility. We compare it to best practices and see how it stacks up against top brands, startups, and enterprises. Then comes usability testing, that real users go through your site while specialists and consultants track pain points.

After gathering insights, the last step is action. You’ll get a clear roadmap for redesign, UI fixes, and UX improvements. Whether you need small tweaks or a full overhaul, an expert agency helps you turn findings into real growth. Because when your user experience works smoothly, customers stay longer and that’s great for business.

The time it takes to complete a UX audit depends on the size and complexity of your website, ecommerce platform, or digital product. A basic review can take a few days, while a deep ui ux audit services package for a large enterprise or b2b platform may take a few weeks.

A top ux audit agency follows a structured process. First, we collect data with studying heatmaps, analytics, and user experience pain points. Next, we analyze your interface, navigation, and UI design to compare it against best practices. If your business needs user testing, that adds more time, but it also gives deeper insights.

Speed matters, but so does accuracy. A rushed audit won’t uncover real issues. That’s why specialists and consultants take the time to deliver a detailed roadmap for redesign, full UI updates, or small tweaks.
